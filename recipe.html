RECIPE

Gado-Gado Recipe

Ingredients

  • 200g fried peanuts
  • 2 pcs white tofu
  • 1 block tempeh
  • 100g bean sprouts
  • 100g spinach
  • 100g long beans
  • 2 boiled eggs
  • 2 potatoes, boiled
  • 3 tbsp sweet soy sauce
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 3 red chilies
  • 1 tsp brown sugar
  • Salt to taste
  • Fried shallots for topping

How to Make

  1. Fry the peanuts until golden brown, then let them cool.
  2. Grind the fried peanuts with garlic, chilies, brown sugar, and salt until smooth.
  3. Add sweet soy sauce and a little warm water to the peanut sauce, mix well.
  4. Fry the tofu and tempeh until golden brown, then cut into cubes.
  5. Blanch the vegetables (sprouts, spinach, long beans) briefly, then drain.
  6. Arrange vegetables, tofu, tempeh, potatoes, and eggs on a plate.
  7. Pour the peanut sauce on top and sprinkle with fried shallots.
  8. Serve with crackers (krupuk) and enjoy!
